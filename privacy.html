Privacy Policy

What we collect

  • Account data — your email address, username/display name, and password hash. Used to create your account and sign you in.
  • Submitted code — every code submission you send to the Arena: the source code, the language, the problem, the test results (passed/failed, runtime, memory), and timestamps. Submissions that pass all tests are stored with a SHA-256 hash chain so later edits or deletions can be detected.
  • Activity data — derived from your submissions: solved counts, per-skill scores, streaks, achievements, and the evidence records you add yourself (projects, assessments). Rate limiting also stores an identity bucket (your user id, or your IP address when not signed in) with a per-minute counter.
  • Local browser storage — for offline and pre-configuration use, progress and self-added evidence may be cached in your browser's localStorage under skillproof_* keys. This stays on your device until you clear it.

Where it is stored

All account, submission, and activity data is stored in Supabase (hosted PostgreSQL with Supabase Auth). Your rows are protected by row-level security: only you can read your submission rows, and client applications cannot insert submissions directly — only the server-side grading function can. Hidden test cases are stored server-side and are never sent to the browser.

To grade a submission, your code is forwarded to an execution sandbox (Judge0 CE) configured by the site operator. The sandbox runs your code and returns its results. Which sandbox instance is used (and therefore which provider processes your code) depends on the operator's deployment.

We do not run advertising trackers or sell data. There are no connected third-party accounts (e.g., GitHub) in the current version.

Who can see what

  • — You: all of your own submissions, code, and evidence (enforced by database policies).
  • — Other users: only the public profile presentation of your aggregate scores, not your code.
  • — The operator: as the Supabase project owner, the operator (and Supabase as processor) can technically access stored data. Accepted submissions are hash-chained, which makes silent tampering detectable — it is not a guarantee against operator access.

Deleting your data

Account and submission deletion is not yet self-service. To request deletion, contact the operator (add your contact email above before publishing). You can clear locally cached data at any time by clearing site data in your browser.

Reminder — This is a template for the site operator to review with a lawyer before use. It does not address GDPR, CCPA, or other jurisdiction-specific requirements, and it may not match your actual deployment (for example, if you disable Supabase or change sandbox providers).
